Why CLA Global

Our member firms are united by a shared spirit of collegiality and exchange, a desire to shape the future of our network, and a drive to accelerate growth for their clients and their people.

CLA Global was founded on 1 July 2022 by CliftonLarsonAllen LLP and S&W (previously Evelyn Partners). Member firms provide services to clients of all sizes, with a particular emphasis on privately owned, fast-growing, mid-market clients with cross-border needs.
